12 questions before you choose your trips

Answer them alone, with a notebook, in 15 minutes. Be honest, nobody else reads it. The answers that surprise you point to the goals that will actually stick.

  1. Which trip in 2026 do I still think about, and why?
  2. Where did I want to go this year but did not, and what stopped me?
  3. Do I want rest, adventure or connection from travel in 2027?
  4. Who do I want to travel with next year?
  5. How much did I spend on travel in 2026, roughly?
  6. What would make a trip feel worth every euro to me?
  7. Which place have I wanted to see since I was a child?
  8. How many days off do I really have in 2027?
  9. What small trip could I take in the next three months?
  10. What do I always overpack or overplan?
  11. Which travel fear could I face next year?
  12. If 2027 goes really well, which trip photo is on my phone by next December?

Plan your travel year in 45 minutes

  1. 10 minutes: look back honestly. Scroll through your 2026 photos and note which trips gave you energy and which only cost money.

Questions

What should go on a travel vision board?

Put the place in your own words with a month, a first step you can do this week, and your reason why. This page has 36 travel goals in 6 groups (dream trips, budget, adventures, close to home, travelling with people you love, travelling better), each with a first step, so you can pick three and make them visible.

Does a travel vision board actually work?

A board does not book flights by itself, but seeing the trip every day keeps it in front of you when you decide what to spend on. The habit that works best is putting it on your phone lock screen.

How many trips should I plan for 2027?

Three is enough: one big trip, one weekend escape and one trip with someone you love. Fewer plans get booked more often.

How do I afford my dream trip in 2027?

Know the real cost, divide it by the months left and save that amount automatically every payday. Add price alerts and points you already collect.
